What happens if you stop paying

We would rather tell you this before you buy than after. If a trial ends or a card stops working, the same ninety-day sequence runs either way, and you get an email at every step naming the exact date of the next one.

  • Days 0 – 30Everything works. A banner appears, and we email you at day 23 and day 29.
  • Days 30 – 60The dashboard locks, but we keep collecting. Pay before day 60 and your history has no gap in it.
  • Days 60 – 90We stop collecting. Everything already recorded is kept, and the missing days show on your graphs as a labelled gap rather than as zeroes.
  • Day 90We permanently delete the account: the analytics database, the account records and the customer record with our payment provider.

Your export works in every one of those phases, including the last day. It is your data, and data portability is not something we switch off for non-payment.

Going over a million

Nothing breaks and nothing is deleted. We email you at 70%, at 85% and at 100%, and the first of those is the one that matters — it reaches you while there is still a month of runway to have a conversation in.

One month over costs you nothing at all. Two consecutive months and we ask you to reply within two weeks so we can move you to something that fits. If nobody replies, the dashboard locks until somebody does — and even then we keep collecting, your settings stay open, and your exports keep working.
