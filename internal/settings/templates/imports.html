Import & export

Bring history in from anywhere, and take everything out at any time. Imported data is stored as roll-up rows that carry every dimension the source reported, so a filter narrows it like native traffic instead of erasing it — and anything the source did not report is shown as a labelled gap rather than counted as zero.
